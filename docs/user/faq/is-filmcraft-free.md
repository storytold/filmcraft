title: Is FilmCraft free?

Yes. FilmCraft is one of the Crafting Apps, which are free, open-source creative tools from the ArtCraft team. It is dual-licensed under MIT or Apache-2.0, at your option. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/storytold/filmcraft).

It runs natively on macOS, Windows and Linux, and in the browser through WebAssembly. See [Install FilmCraft](../getting-started/install.md) for the downloads.

FilmCraft is independent of Adobe. It contains no Adobe code, icons, images, presets or LUTs, and it is not affiliated with Adobe.

It is in alpha. The project's own estimate is that it is about 56% of the way to being ready to replace Premiere Pro on real projects, so check that it covers what you need. Details are in the [status section of the README](https://github.com/storytold/filmcraft#status).
