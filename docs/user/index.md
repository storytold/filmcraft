FilmCraft is a video editor for people who know Adobe Premiere Pro. It has the same kind of panels, workspaces, tools and shortcuts, and it is free and open source.

It has Source and Program monitors, a multi-track timeline, three-point editing, the usual trim modes, a Lumetri-style color panel with scopes, an audio mixer, titles and captions, and export. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux, and in a browser. Its video codecs are its own code, with no FFmpeg inside. FilmCraft is in alpha: it is young and moving fast, so expect gaps.
